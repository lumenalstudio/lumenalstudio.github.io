Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Lumenal Studio LLC ("Lumenal Studio," "we," "us") is an independent game studio based in Washington State, USA. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, our games and our community spaces, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect as little as we can, we never sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our website

lumenalstudio.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical information such as your IP address and browser type to operate and secure the service. Those logs are handled under GitHub's privacy statement; we do not receive or use them.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Our games

Our games are sold through Steam, which is operated by Valve Corporation. Valve handles your Steam account, purchases and payment details under Valve's privacy policy; we never see your payment information. Through Steam we may receive:

  • Your Steam ID and display name, so the game can identify you, save your progress and show your name to other players in multiplayer sessions.
  • Network information needed for multiplayer, such as connection details exchanged through Steam's networking services so that players can join each other's games.
  • Aggregated sales and usage statistics that Steam provides to developers, which do not identify individual players.

Game saves are stored on your own computer, and in Steam Cloud if you have it turned on. Our games are still in development; before each game is released, we will update this policy with exactly what that game collects, including any crash reports or gameplay statistics, and why.

Community spaces

Our Discord server is operated by Discord, under Discord's privacy policy. We can see what you post there and your Discord username, as can other members, and we use it only to run and moderate the community.

How we use and share information

  • We use information only to run our games, website and community, to respond to you, to keep things secure and to meet legal obligations.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We share information only with the services that run things for us (such as Valve, GitHub and Discord), or when the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Union, the United Kingdom and some US states, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how we use it. To make a request, email us at the address below. We will respond within the time the law requires and will not treat you differently for asking.

Children

Our website and games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, such as when a game is released, and change the effective date above.
