What we make

Worlds worth wandering into.

We make games for players who like to explore, notice things, and feel a little uneasy doing it. Every project starts with a place and asks what happens if you stay there too long.

Spaces that shift

Rule-built environments that are different every time you step through the door, so no two visits are quite the same.

Better together

Cooperative play where you explore with friends, split up when you shouldn't, and compare notes on what you saw.

Atmosphere first

Light, sound and scale do the heavy lifting. Built to run smoothly on the hardware players already own.

The studio

Small team, careful work.

Lumenal Studio is a small independent developer based in Washington State. We'd rather ship fewer things and get them right: tight design, honest pricing, and games that respect your time.
